# Aerospace Dynamics MATLAB Simulations

MATLAB-based modeling and simulation of aerospace multi-body dynamics and orbital mechanics.

## Projects

### 1. Fixed-Wing Aircraft with Suspended Load (Multi-Body Dynamics)
- Derived full 3D nonlinear equations of motion using **Lagrangian formalism**
- Modeled dynamic coupling between fixed-wing aircraft and suspended payload
- Highly nonlinear system containing 25+ trigonometric terms per state variable
- Simulated flight stability and pendulum effects during maneuvers
- Included moment of inertia measurement setup for UAV

**Key Skills Demonstrated:**  
Multi-body dynamics • Lagrangian mechanics • Nonlinear equations of motion • MATLAB simulation • Flight dynamics

### 2. Earth Gravity Assist Maneuver
- Orbital mechanics simulation of gravity assist trajectory
- Visualization of spacecraft path around Earth
